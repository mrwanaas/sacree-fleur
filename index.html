Our Story

A little corner
of Paris since 2010

Tucked away at the foot of the Butte Montmartre, a five-minute stroll from Sacré-Cœur, Sacrée Fleur has been a neighbourhood institution since chef-owner Stéphane Ducreux opened its doors in 2010.

With only ten tables, every evening is an intimate affair. We source the finest cuts of Charolais beef, treat them with nothing but heat, salt, and time, and serve them sizzling on volcanic hot stone so you finish the cooking exactly as you like it.

There are no vegetarian options and no compromises — only honest French cuisine, carefully chosen wines, and the kind of warmth that makes you want to come back.
